About Kalmorn Tech

A Canadian technology company building software that makes a difference in healthcare and the environment.

Our Mission

At Kalmorn Tech, we believe technology should solve real-world problems. Our mission is to build innovative software products and deliver expert consulting that creates tangible impact in the industries we serve.

We focus on two domains where technology can truly transform outcomes: healthcare and the environment. Through our products and consulting practice, we help organizations embrace the future with confidence.

Our Vision

We envision a world where technology seamlessly bridges the gap between complex challenges and elegant solutions. Kalmorn Tech aims to be a leading force in developing purpose-driven software that enhances clinical workflows and advances environmental stewardship.

With ambitious product roadmaps and a growing consulting practice, we're positioned to scale our impact across Canada and beyond.

Meet the Founder

Kalu Ejibe — enterprise transformation leader and product builder.

Built Across Three Continents

Kalmorn Tech is grounded in fourteen years of technology delivery across three continents. Kalu Ejibe's career spans engineering banking applications at Fidelity Bank, training process-excellence champions at the Central Bank of Nigeria, and directing digital transformation programmes across Nigeria, the UK, and Ghana — before moving into Canadian enterprise.

Delivering across that range of regulatory environments, industries, and infrastructure realities shaped the conviction behind every product Kalmorn ships: the best technology meets people where they are — the clinic room, the drill site, the spreadsheet people already live in.

Enterprise Rigour, Founder Speed

Kalu leads IT demand and resource management in the enterprise PMO at ENMAX, one of Alberta's largest utilities — managing annual IT investments of $80M–$130M CAD and building the integrated planning model behind them. Before that: leading platform development for an AI learning company, and a decade of transformation work on three continents.

Kalmorn Tech is where that discipline meets speed. Every product — VOSS, Drillr, GRYD, Tymr — was conceived, architected, and shipped by a founder who writes the code, talks to the users, and stands behind the result. The enterprise work keeps the products rigorous; the founder work keeps them moving.
