Frequently Asked Questions

1) Microphone is not working

  • Open iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone
  • Enable microphone access for VOSS
  • Restart the app and try again

2) Speech recognition is not working

  • Open Settings > Privacy & Security > Speech Recognition
  • Enable access for VOSS
  • Confirm your internet connection is stable
  • Retry after relaunching the app

3) I’m seeing network/server errors

  • Check Wi-Fi/cellular connection
  • Update to the latest app version
  • Wait briefly and retry
  • If it persists, email support with details

4) How should I interpret “Possible Explanations”?

Possible Explanations are informational suggestions generated from provided content.

They are for decision support and wellness context, not definitive conclusions or professional medical advice.
