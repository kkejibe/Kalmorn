iOS App for Clinicians & Patients

VOSS

An AI-powered ambient scribe for clinicians with a dedicated patient mode. VOSS records doctor–patient encounters, transcribes them on-device, and generates structured SOAP notes, differential diagnoses, and patient-friendly summaries — so clinicians spend less time documenting and more time with patients. Patients get their own mode with an AI health companion to ask follow-up questions and understand their visit in plain language.

  • ✓ Dual modes — Clinician for ambient documentation, Patient for understanding visits
  • ✓ Ambient recording of doctor–patient encounters with pause/resume
  • ✓ On-device transcription via Apple Speech — audio never leaves the iPhone by default
  • ✓ AI-generated SOAP notes with specialty-aware clinical templates
  • ✓ Differential diagnoses with likelihood, rationale, and red-flag indicators
  • ✓ Patient mode: plain-language visit summaries plus an AI health companion for follow-up questions
  • ✓ Privacy-first: zero-retention AI processing, encrypted in transit, audio deleted immediately

Web Platform

Drillr

Professional borehole logging software for environmental geologists who work where the internet doesn't. Drillr is an offline-first field platform that captures detailed stratigraphic data, generates client-ready PDF and Excel reports in one tap, and syncs to the cloud the moment you're back online — across iPad, iPhone, Android, and desktop.

  • ✓ Offline-first PWA — log boreholes in the field with zero connectivity, sync automatically when back online
  • ✓ Full stratigraphic logging: lithology, intervals, inclusions, chemistry, and photos per layer
  • ✓ Multiple classification standards built in — USCS, CSSC, BS 5930, ASTM D2488, plus custom codes for team standardization
  • ✓ One-tap professional PDF reports with graphic stratigraphic columns and lithology patterns
  • ✓ Excel export (GRYD-compatible) for downstream analysis and lab integration
  • ✓ Munsell color picker, SPT blow-count capture, and GPS coordinates with UTM ↔ WGS84 conversion
  • ✓ Interactive Mapbox-powered project maps with borehole plotting
  • ✓ Side-by-side log comparison across boreholes in a project
  • ✓ Installs like a native app — no App Store required, no IT approval needed

Excel Add-in

GRYD

Environmental intelligence, simplified. GRYD lives inside the Excel workbooks and lab results environmental professionals already use — validating data, cross-referencing regulatory guidelines, and flagging exceedances automatically. No uploads. No exports. No new platform to learn.

  • ✓ Automated exceedance detection across soil, groundwater, and surface water
  • ✓ Multi-tiered guideline frameworks with site-specific background calibration
  • ✓ Forensic hydrogeochemistry — Piper, Stiff, Gibbs, Schoeller, Wilcox, PAH cross-plots, and source discriminator diagrams
  • ✓ Integrated GIS and plume mapping with excavation soil-volume calculations
  • ✓ Configurable non-detect (ND) policies and custom guideline overrides
  • ✓ Compliance-ready reports with visual summaries and defensible audit trails

GRYD Highlights

  • End-to-end pipeline — field sampling through lab results to regulatory-ready submission
  • Built for wellsite assessments, environmental site assessments, and contaminated site remediation
  • Statistical analysis engine purpose-built for environmental datasets and non-detect handling
  • Instant flagging for hydrocarbon exceedances, salinity, and heavy metal contamination
  • Audit-ready outputs with traceable data lineage for regulatory submission
