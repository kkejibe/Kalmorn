02 / Environmental intelligence

GRYD

Make environmental data work for you.

An Excel add-in for environmental teams working with soil, groundwater, and surface water data. GRYD helps organize laboratory results, screen against guideline frameworks, and prepare clear analysis within your workbook.

  • Lab data normalization, analyte mapping, and unit conversion
  • Guideline screening with versioned runs and reviewable exceedances
  • Environmental charts, mapping, and site-specific analysis
  • Designed for assessment, remediation, and closure workflows

03 / Field documentation

Drillr

Good records start in the field.

Borehole logging for environmental and geotechnical teams working beyond reliable connectivity. Capture stratigraphy, monitoring wells, samples, and field observations, then prepare the records your project needs.

  • Offline borehole logging with synchronization when connectivity returns
  • Voice-assisted logging with structured drafts for your review
  • Monitoring well construction, sample records, and QA/QC information
  • PDF logs, cross-sections, site plans, and spreadsheet exports

04 / Environmental project management

Tymr

Know where your project stands.

An environmental project management system focused on the people, time, and costs behind delivery. Tymr helps environmental teams understand capacity and project effort across fieldwork, analysis, and reporting.

  • Draft time entries from connected work tools for team review
  • Project cost, capacity, and utilization visibility
  • Project health and portfolio scenarios to support planning
  • Reviewable time records with privacy controls
